📲 Quick 3-Step Setup Guide *follow this step for better data sync

To sync your real phone data automatically with the OutLoot dashboard, follow these quick steps:

1. Install the Tracker: Download the free, open-source ActivityWatch app from the Google Play Store here.
2. Turn On 2 Settings: Open the app and click GRANT to enable these two required permissions:
  • Usage Access (Allows it to count screen minutes)
  • Exact Alarms (Allows it to update the charts automatically)
3. Try Again: Make sure the app says Server status: Running, then go back to your browser page and tap the Sync Android Digital Wellbeing button! Your live screen time will instantly load.
